# spotify liner notes

a little tool that reads your spotify playlists and tells you stuff about your music taste. moods, keys, genres, which label you keep coming back to, all that.

how to use it: export your playlists with exportify (free, watsonbox.github.io/exportify), open spotify_liner_notes.html in a browser, and drop the csv files in. you can select them all at once.

thats it. everything runs in the browser, nothing gets uploaded anywhere, your listening history never leaves your machine.
